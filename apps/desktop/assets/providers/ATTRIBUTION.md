# Provider logo sources

- `chatgpt.png`: White ChatGPT Blossom from OpenAI's official [`openai-cookbook` asset](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/5986832a554169dc87285b1b0b396941f235a62e/examples/voice_solutions/realtime_translation_guide/livekit-translation-demo/public/brand/chatgpt-blossom-white.svg), rasterized at 192 px with the unused outer whitespace reduced for the small provider tab; the mark itself is unchanged.
- `claude.png`: Claude spark from the Claude wordmark on https://claude.com (the same mark as its favicon), in Claude orange `#D97757`, rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `antigravity.png`: https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.png
- `opencode-go.png`: https://opencode.ai/favicon-96x96-v3.png
- `openrouter.png`: https://openrouter.ai/brand/logos/transparent/glyph/png/glyph-cloud@2x.png
- `deepseek.png`: DeepSeek whale from the favicon on https://www.deepseek.com (`/favicon.ico`, 225 px), trimmed and resized to 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `copilot.png`: GitHub Copilot mark from GitHub's own Octicons set ([`primer/octicons` `icons/copilot-24.svg`](https://github.com/primer/octicons), MIT), drawn in white and rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `cursor.png`: Cursor cube from Cursor's brand kit on https://cursor.com/brand (`cursor-brand-assets.zip`, `General Logos/Cube/SVG/CUBE_2D_DARK.svg`), drawn in white and rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `kimi.png`, `kimi-light-theme.png`: Kimi "K" mark from the `KforKimi_f` icon on https://www.kimi.com (`statics.moonshot.cn/kimi-web-seo/assets/kimi.icon-*.js`), the K drawn white (black for light themes) and the dot in Kimi blue `#1783FF` as on https://www.kimi.com/pwa-192.png, rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `zai.png`: The Z of z.ai's site logo (https://z.ai, `z-cdn.chatglm.cn/z-ai/static/logo.svg`), without its tile, rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `xai.png`: xAI mark from the site icon on https://x.ai (`/icon.png`, 512 px), the black mark turned white without its tile, resized to 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `minimax.png`: MiniMax wave from MiniMax's own repository ([`MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-01` `figures/minimax.svg`](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-01)), rasterized at 192 px with a small margin to match the other tab icons.
- `mimo.png`: The M of the MIMO wordmark in the favicon on https://platform.xiaomimimo.com (`/favicon.png`), drawn in white and resized to 192 px with a margin to match the other tab icons.
