# Service logo sources

Logos the API keys page shows for services the app does not otherwise
track. Each was taken from the company's own site and placed on a 192 px
transparent square with a small margin, the mark itself unchanged. Services
the app tracks reuse the logos in `../providers`.

- `gemini.png`: Gemini sparkle, the site icon of https://gemini.google.com (`gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_*.png`).
- `mistral.png`: Mistral "M" from the header of https://mistral.ai (inline SVG), rasterized.
- `groq.png`: https://groq.com/favicon.svg, rasterized.
- `perplexity.png`: https://www.perplexity.ai/favicon.svg, rasterized.
- `qwen.png`: Qwen Chat site icon on https://chat.qwen.ai (`assets.alicdn.com/g/qwenweb/qwen-chat-fe/*/favicon.png`).
- `cohere.png`: https://cohere.com/apple-touch-icon.png
- `together.png`: Together AI touch icon on https://www.together.ai (`*_touchicon.png`).
- `fireworks.png`: https://fireworks.ai/icon0.svg, rasterized.
- `cerebras.png`: Cerebras site icon on https://www.cerebras.ai (`cdn.sanity.io/images/e4qjo92p/production/*-967x967.png`).
- `huggingface.png`: https://huggingface.co/front/assets/huggingface_logo-noborder.svg, rasterized.
- `elevenlabs.png`: https://elevenlabs.io/icon.svg, rasterized.

The rest are from the [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)
collection (`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.1, MIT), rasterized the same
way; one-color marks are kept white and drawn black on a light theme:
ai21, assemblyai, azure, baiducloud, bedrock, bfl, deepinfra, doubao, exa,
fal, firecrawl, github, hunyuan, hyperbolic, ideogram, jina, kling, luma,
meta, nebius, novita, nvidia, ollama, poe, reka, replicate, runway,
sambanova, siliconcloud, stability, stepfun, tavily, upstage, venice, vercel,
vertexai, voyage, wandb, workersai. The marks themselves belong to their
companies.
